How to Print

1
Tap your DNSC ID on the kiosk's RFID reader. Your e-wallet opens automatically.
2
Upload a PDF from your phone. The file stays on your device — only the pages you choose are sent.
3
Pick pages and set paper (Short/Long), mode (B&W/Color), and number of copies.
4
Confirm & print. Credits are deducted only after the job finishes. SMS receipt arrives on your phone.
5
Collect your pages from the Epson L3210 tray below the kiosk.
Pricing: Short ₱2/page, Long ₱3/page, Color +₱1/page
